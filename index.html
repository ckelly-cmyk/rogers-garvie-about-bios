Associate Attorney

Kennedy Templeton Bio v1

Kennedy Templeton joined Rogers & Garvie, LLC as an associate in August 2026. Kennedy graduated with honors from the University of Wyoming College of Law in May 2026. She previously earned her bachelor's degree in criminal justice and history, summa cum laude, from the University of Wyoming in 2023.

During law school, Kennedy was awarded the Bernstein Scholarship, which provided her the opportunity to travel to Cambodia for two months where she worked with a non-governmental organization providing legal services to women and children. She also studied international criminal law abroad through an externship at Trinity College in Cambridge, England, and The Hague in the Netherlands.

Kennedy was the recipient of the Outstanding Student Award for the International Human Rights Clinic and served as a staff member of the Wyoming Law Review. She was also a member of the University of Wyoming's Jessup International Law Moot Court team, competing in the world's largest international moot court competition. Her practical legal experience includes serving as a judicial intern for the Honorable Misha Westby of the Second Judicial District Court of Wyoming in Albany County and as an intern with The Spence Law Firm in Jackson, Wyoming.

Kennedy is licensed to practice in the State of Wyoming and the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming.

Originally from Bakersfield, California, Kennedy now enjoys calling Wyoming home. When she's away from the office, Kennedy enjoys traveling, cooking, gardening, reading, and spending time with family and friends. She especially enjoys coaching local youth sports and watching her son play hockey. If she isn't at the rink or in the kitchen, there's a good chance she's somewhere with a book and a cup of coffee in hand.

Staff

Law Clerk

Maggie Asay

Maggie Asay joined Rogers & Garvie, LLC as a law clerk in September 2026. Maggie is a Wyoming native proud to be raising her family in the state she calls home. Maggie earned her bachelor's degree in Legal Science from Purdue University Global before attending the University of Wyoming College of Law.

During law school, Maggie gained experience working with clients and appearing in court, seeing clients across Wyoming, and traveling to Europe to study International Human Rights. She began working with the Defender Aid Clinic as a legal assistant the summer after her first year of law school and continued with the clinic as its Student Director through graduation.

Maggie's time with the Defender Aid Clinic gave her a sense of purpose, direction, created a deep passion for criminal defense, and a commitment to advocating for those in need. That experience affirmed her desire to build a career in criminal law, family law, mediation, and serving the people of Wyoming.

Co-Founder & Member

Grant Rogers

Grant Rogers is a co-founder and member of Rogers & Garvie, LLC in Laramie, Wyoming. He represents businesses and property owners in transactions, disputes, and litigation, with a focus on civil litigation, business and commercial matters, environmental and regulatory advising, and real property. He also serves Wyoming individuals and families in family law, criminal defense, and probate and estate planning.

Civil LitigationBusiness & CommercialEnvironmental & RegulatoryReal PropertyFamily LawCriminal DefenseProbate & Estate Planning

Grant graduated with honors from the University of Wyoming College of Law in 2020, where he served on the board of the Wyoming Law Review. While in law school, he was a judicial intern for District Judge Timothy C. Day of the Ninth Judicial District of Wyoming. He also interned for the Albany County Attorney's Office, the Environment and Natural Resources Division of the U.S. Department of Justice, the Spence Law Firm, and the UW Defender Aid Clinic. Before law school, he completed degrees in Political Science and Economics at the University of Wyoming.

After law school, Grant practiced with Davis & Cannon, LLP in Sheridan. He opened his own practice as The Rogers Law Firm in January 2024, and in February 2025 joined with Thomas Garvie to form Rogers & Garvie, LLC.

Grant is licensed to practice in the State of Wyoming and the United States District Court for the District of Wyoming.

Grant was born and raised in Jackson, Wyoming, and has deep roots with the state. He spends his free time climbing, running, reading, and baking, and is all ears for a good story.

Co-Founder

Thomas Garvie

Thomas is a Wyoming native and a graduate of the University of Wyoming where he earned a Bachelor's degree in Political Science before attending the University of Wyoming College of Law. While studying at the College of Law, he focused on gaining as much practical experience as possible.

During that time, Thomas served as a Judicial Intern to Wyoming Supreme Court Justice Keith G. Kautz, interned at the Albany County Attorney's Office as well as the Hirst Applegate Law Firm, and spent his final year working as a student-attorney for the College of Law's Defender Aid Clinic.

Thomas' dream upon graduating was to become a Public Defender to help protect those without the knowledge to defend themselves nor the financial ability to hire an attorney. He spent his first years of practice with the Wyoming Public Defender's Office, representing 200-300 individuals a year, each facing serious criminal charges.

Not every attorney likes to go to court, but there is no place Thomas is more comfortable. While he meets each new client with compassion and empathy, he understands that honed skills and the willingness to fight are what will make a difference in their lives.
